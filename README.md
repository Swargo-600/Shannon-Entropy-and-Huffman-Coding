## Abstract

This research paper explores Shannon Entropy and Huffman Coding, focusing on information theory, greedy algorithms, coding efficiency, and their applications in data compression. It also compares static and adaptive Huffman coding to understand their advantages, limitations, and practical applications.

## Objectives

- To understand Shannon Entropy and information theory.
- To explain the working principle of Huffman Coding.
- To explore the role of greedy algorithms in data compression.
- To compare static and adaptive Huffman coding.
- To examine the practical applications of these techniques.
